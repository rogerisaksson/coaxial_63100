- Three faults in the driving, none in the form: the knee landed at 15 deg
  and driven to the strut's 5 under load within 0.15 s, where a woman lands

  before it; the swing's knee driven to a setpoint and braked into its
  landing, where a swing is a pendulum's. The standing twentieths cost alike.
