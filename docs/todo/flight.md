# Flight pages

- **The crafts' continuum** (aircraft.md, aerobat.md): the aerobat's stunts
  switch - a stunt or not, the waterfall's and the inverted nose at -1.5
  and -0.5, the airborne latch, the burn -, as do the helicopter's and the
  ornithopter's laws; the line's lean is its asked speed's derivative
  clipped (`aerokin.lean`). DOD: no branch on a threshold in the three
  laws; at each stage change the angular acceleration and the controls'
  step within the stage's own; the three flown whole on their instruments
  and in Dryden's scales.
- **The aerobat's aerodynamics from its form** (aerobat.md, aircraft.md):
  the aerobat's tail arm and wing area are typed in crafts.toml
  (`tail_arm_m`, `wing_span_m` x `wing_chord_m`), not the drawn Extra's
  (`form`). DOD: both derived from `form` by `machine.aircraft.compile`;
  the aerobat's card flies whole, the knife pass's tip within 2 cm of its
  4 cm over the runway.
- **The aerobat in wind** (aerobat.md): the aerobat's page strikes in kept
  and turbulent winds - its knife pass, its final, its course, before the
  deck and on it. DOD: the aerobat's page on the tour and in kept winds,
  none struck.
- **A spent pack mid-turn-over** (aerobat.md): the quad's pack spent in
  its roll or its flip, its way down begins upside down and strikes. DOD:
  the quad's pack spent anywhere on its card, the quad lands.
- **The courses on what the crafts have** (aerobat.md): the quad's course
  is planned on a fixed share of its pull (`course.PULL`), the
  helicopter's and the ornithopter's on 2.2 g (`course.TWIN_SPHERE`), not
  on what their laws' corrections leave. DOD: each course on as much as
  its law's corrections leave, its line laid for it, no gate struck in the
  kept winds and the turbulent seeds.
- **The propellers' inertia and grip** (aircraft.md): the helicopter's and
  the ornithopter's pages turn the quad's rotor (`pull.ROTOR`: the
  63100's can and a 16x14), their suites ideal rotors, and their discs'
  thrust falls with the air through them by the quad's 14 in pitch
  whatever their collective (`quad.inflow`). DOD: each craft's rotor its
  own inertia, its thrust by its blades' pitch against its inflow, page
  and suites alike.
- **The aerobat's control at speed** (aerobat.md): out of a turn the
  aerobat rolls past level, the more the faster it flies, and straight in
  gusts its bank spreads. DOD: out of a turn within 2 deg of level at any
  speed; straight in gusts within 5.
- **The aerobat's landing** (aerobat.md): in gusts the aerobat's touches
  land downwind of the centreline, its float drifting in the floor's
  crosswind. DOD: a touch under 0.5 m/s within 0.5 m of the centreline,
  stopped on the runway, still or gusty.
