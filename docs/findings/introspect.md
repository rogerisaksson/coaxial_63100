# Her layers live

`machine.introspect` serves her loop's three layers each law pass - latent
(her state's rows by mode, her latent out), impedance (the modal ask, the
step's ask), wrench (the step's answer: tau, qacc, bears, cop, ..) - as JSON
lines on 127.0.0.1 to every client attached (`show_humanoid --telemetry
PORT`, `python -m machine.introspect --attach PORT`, `introspect.read`).
Every field named from her code's own constants (`introspect.layout`: the
run of module-level slices ending at a vector's size, in rising offsets -
the ask's 37 to `modal.ASK`, latent out's 14 to `latent.OUT`, a row's 12 to
`ortho.P`, each gapless), her modes and manifolds by `ortho.MODES` and
`ortho.MANIFOLDS`. Paced on her walk, the fastest of interleaved 1 500-pass
chunks: 615 us a pass without the hook, 604 with it and none attached (a
check at its first line), 875 with a client reading every law pass - the
snapshot and its JSON 0.26 ms a pass, under real time's 1 000. A slow client
is dropped behind, never waited on (64 queued).

## Reduced for a reader with little room

`machine.watch` runs her analyzers (`machine/analyzers`, a file each:
`laid(meta)`, `step(state, snap) -> events`) over the snapshots and hands
on only their events; its folder read again each second, a file saved
reloaded, one removed dropped, one that raises told and held off while the
rest fire on. Between snapshots `introspect.Tap` holds every pass's
fastest joint (a peak detector): a snapshot a law pass alone read the
tip's fastest 249.7 deg/s where every pass's 270.7; held, each closed
phase's fastest is every pass's to the digit (kneel right elbow 333.1, tip
right gripper 270.7 - the probes' reduction, test_introspect). Triggers
are her body's own - a phase's flip, a doing back after a blip under her
natural time (chatter), her soles' share crossing half and holding there
her natural time (a landing's bounce about half fired 15 times in 0.1 s
undebounced), a fall, a joint past its drive's rated speed, a say refused,
an errand's phase. A trigger marked capture is a scope's single shot:
half her leg's swing before and after (`takes.ENTER_S`, 0.88 s) written
whole to build/telemetry, held off till written. On the page as the user
runs it (`--telemetry`, the prompt typed): 'lay on floor' 8 events in 16
s, the probe's phases and spans (kneel 2.502 s, tip 1.5); 'do a backflip'
through the local model, a fall at 3.011 s captured, 442 snapshots
