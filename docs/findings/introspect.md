# Her layers live

`machine.introspect` serves her loop's three layers each law pass - latent
(her state's rows by mode, her latent out), impedance (the modal ask, the
step's ask), wrench (the step's answer: tau, qacc, bears, cop, ..) - as JSON
lines on 127.0.0.1 to every client attached (`show_humanoid --telemetry
PORT`, `python -m machine.introspect --attach PORT`, `introspect.read`).
Every field named from her code's own constants (`introspect.layout`: the
run of module-level slices ending at a vector's size, in rising offsets -
the ask's 37 to `modal.ASK`, latent out's 14 to `latent.OUT`, a row's 12 to
`ortho.P`, each gapless), a field's entries by the tuple its own definition
counts (`introspect.counted`: `len(SKIN)` in TOUCH's, through `latent.N` to
`ortho.MODES`), none named from its width alone. Paced on her walk, the
fastest of interleaved 1 500-pass chunks: 615 us a pass without the hook,
604 with it and none attached (a check at its first line), 875 with a
client reading every law pass - the snapshot and its JSON 0.26 ms a pass,
under real time's 1 000. A slow client is dropped behind, never waited on
(64 queued).

## Reduced for a reader with little room

its folder read again each second, a file saved reloaded, one removed

## Gated

`machine.gate` records her streams on their own clocks while a gate is
armed (`streams.STREAMS`): the commands heard; her edge's reads - IMU,
encoders and drives' torques each pass, soles and skin each law pass; her
things at her registry's refresh (9 ms); her law's latent, impedance and
wrench each law pass; her drives each pass; the world the pass left; her
wear as told; her events. A trigger - an analyzer's event named, else one
marked capture - takes half her leg's swing before it and after
(`takes.ENTER_S`, 0.88 s); the gate armed again till its count, a trigger
in an open window held off. Its file is gate after gate: a metadata line
(the trigger's name, tag and event, the window, each stream's maker and
words, kind, period, the most it fits, its fields' offsets and the names
their entries are), its records in time, the streams interleaved and one
instant's in her pass's order, a trailer (counts, held off, a cut).
Against a harness recording each subsystem where it runs - her edge's
reads, her law's control, her drives, the world's step, her registry's
look, her wear, the commands - every gated section of the 11 streams the
same to the bit in two gates of her floor lie; of 10 bugs planted 8 were
caught, the 2 passing equivalent (her law's ask and answer new each law
pass), a reused buffer aliased (her latent state) caught (test_gate). On
the page, held against the snapshot stream of the same run: angles, peaks
between snapshots, latent rows, the modal ask and the step's answer the
same in 1 511 snapshots of three gates of 'lay on floor'. A gate is 16 MB:
1 761 records of each kHz stream, 587 of each law stream. Its JSON encoded
beside her loop held her passes 0.4 s a gate; a writer's process fed
through a pipe takes it in 36 ms. Armed, a pass costs 15 % more
in-process; the page's pace 0.74 of real time idle, 0.58 gating, her
world's steps unchanged. Her page's worker is a daemon process: a
multiprocessing writer raised in her pass and ended the page; a fault of
her telemetry is now told to its clients, its gates dropped. A small tour
of the cabin with a gate armed from a shell: 93 lines streamed in 90 s, a
second behind her; her fall at 47.784 s standing up from the floor gated,
5 671 records, 11.6 MB - her law's and drives' streams end at the fall,
her edge's reads and the world run on. Her fingers, held a fist at 80 deg
(her build), were laid at the stand's 18 and snapped in at 893.5 deg/s on
every lay, her wrists held at 0 laid at 5 at 69.1 - every scene's first
phase's fastest joint in the tour's stream; the world lays a pose within
each joint's stops now (`physics.World.reset`): 0.4 and 3.8 deg/s, her
walk as it was (0.70 m/s, 408 J/m, 2.50 off a woman's); her held standing
jump steps 0.59 m from its landing where 0.24 - its crouch taps her soles
